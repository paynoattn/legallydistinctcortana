Five names for the assistant

A free, local-first voice assistant for Windows. Each concept is a name you say out loud, a mark that sits in the bottom-right corner and reacts while you speak, an app icon, and a 16 px tray icon. Marks are shown on Windows 11 light (#F3F3F3) and dark (#202020) surfaces at real size.

Recommendation: Lampion. The clearest mark at 16 px, the most natural voice-reactive motion (a light that brightens when you speak), and a story that is the product: a small light of your own, kept at home. Runner-up: Avocet, which has the lowest name risk and a sharp triage story. Full reasoning is in README.md.
